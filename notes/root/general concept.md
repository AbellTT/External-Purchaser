Platform Overview

The Stationery Procurement Platform is a web-based procurement system designed to help organizations such as schools, universities, government offices, NGOs, and private companies purchase stationery products at lower prices by combining their purchasing power. Instead of each organization buying independently from retailers or middlemen, the platform groups orders into procurement baskets (weekly, monthly, or six-month), allowing large bulk purchases directly from wholesale suppliers in Merkato. As more organizations participate, the total order quantity increases, enabling better wholesale pricing, and the estimated price for participants decreases dynamically throughout the basket period.

In addition to collective purchasing, the platform provides valuable market intelligence by tracking historical price trends over time. Organizations can view how prices have changed over the past two years, identify seasonal price fluctuations, and make informed purchasing decisions based on historical market behavior. This helps procurement officers determine not only what to buy, but also the best time to buy, potentially saving significant amounts on large procurement budgets.

For organizations that require products immediately, the platform also offers a direct purchasing option, allowing them to place individual orders without waiting for a basket to close while still benefiting from competitive pricing. Throughout the purchasing process, users can monitor basket progress, estimated savings, order status, and delivery updates through an intuitive dashboard.

Behind the scenes, the platform provides administrators with a comprehensive management system for handling organizations, suppliers, products, baskets, pricing, procurement activities, deliveries, and reporting. By digitizing and centralizing the procurement process, the platform aims to reduce purchasing costs, improve transparency, simplify procurement planning, and create a more efficient connection between institutional buyers and wholesale suppliers.
